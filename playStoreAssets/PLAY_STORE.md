## Full description

```
Mathtizzy helps you build real math-fact fluency—not just “got it right,” but got it right quickly.

Practice starts with tiny totals and grows through addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division. Each fact is tracked on its own. When a fact is answered correctly and fast enough, often enough, it turns green. Fill the whole grid and level up—or stay and keep sharpening.

WHY IT WORKS
• Starts easy and gets harder as skills grow
• Focuses on shaky facts instead of endless easy ones
• Green means fluent: accurate and quick
• Missed facts are read aloud and highlighted so kids can see and hear the correct answer, then read along

LEVELS
From Tiny Totals through Times Tables, Fact Mixer, timed Speed Facts, and Mathlete challenges—twelve levels that grow with your child.

FEATURES
• Multiple players on one device—each with their own progress
• On-screen number pad (great for phones and tablets)
• Streaks, accuracy, and a clear mastery map
• Adjustable “maximum time” so fluency matches your child’s pace
• Optional music and spoken corrections
• Progress saved on the device—no account required

Built for practice that feels like a game, with feedback that actually teaches.

Music by Fesliyan Studios.
```
